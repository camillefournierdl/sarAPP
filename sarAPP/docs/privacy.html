sarapp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

In short: sarapp does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter is stored only in a local database on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers, and no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

What data the app stores

sarapp is a daily journaling and cycle-tracking app. Depending on which modules you enable, it may store information you choose to enter, such as mood, emotions, sleep, weather, health status, work, sports, leisure, food, symptoms, libido, sex, water intake, notes, and menstrual cycle/period dates, plus any custom questions you create.

This data is written to a local SQLite database that lives entirely on your device. sarapp has no backend server, and the developer has no way to access, view, or receive any of it.

No data transmission

The app does not make network requests. It contains no analytics SDKs, crash reporters, advertising libraries, or any other third-party services that would send information off your device. The in-app "Analytics" screen computes statistics (e.g. correlations between tracked factors) entirely on-device, using only your own locally stored entries.

Exporting your data

You can optionally export your entries as a CSV file using the built-in export feature. This is entirely user-initiated: the file is handed to your device's native share sheet, and you choose where it goes (e.g. saving it, emailing it, or sending it via another app). The developer has no visibility into, or control over, this action.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, it is scheduled locally by your device's operating system. There is no push notification server involved, and no data is sent anywhere to make reminders work.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored locally, uninstalling the app or clearing its app data/storage from your device's settings permanently deletes everything sarapp has stored.

Children's privacy

sarapp does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, since it does not collect or transmit information at all — all data stays on the user's own device.

Open source

sarapp's source code is publicly available for review at github.com/camillefournierdl/sarapp, so you can verify these claims yourself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Since the app collects no data, changes are expected to be rare and minor.
